# AdventureWorks Workforce Story Presentation

## Project Overview

This project turns an HR Power BI dashboard into an executive-style workforce story for AdventureWorks leadership.

The presentation is designed for a senior audience after a company merger. It focuses on workforce structure, hiring volatility, department concentration, tenure, mobility, diversity, data limitations, and practical recommendations.

## Main Deliverables

- `AdventureWorks Workforce Story Presentation.pptx`: executive presentation deck.
- `AdventureWorks HR Dashboard.pbix`: supporting Power BI dashboard.
- `AdventureWorks HR Dashboard Export.pdf`: exported dashboard preview.
- `story_summary.md`: concise explanation of the presentation storyline, findings, and recommendations.

## Business Context

AdventureWorks leadership needs a clear view of workforce trends after a merger. The goal is not only to show dashboard numbers, but to turn them into a business story with risks, implications, and recommended actions.

## Presentation Story

The deck follows a simple executive flow:

1. Merger context creates a need for workforce visibility.
2. Dashboard findings reveal hiring, structure, tenure, mobility, and diversity patterns.
3. Workforce concentration creates operational risk.
4. Limited mobility and missing exit data reduce HR decision quality.
5. Leadership should rebalance headcount, strengthen internal mobility, improve diversity, and improve HR data completeness.

## Key Findings

- AdventureWorks has **290 active employees** in the HR dataset.
- More than **60% of employees** sit in Production.
- Several departments have fewer than 10 employees.
- Hiring activity is volatile, including a major hiring spike in 2009.
- Sales has lower tenure than most departments.
- Internal mobility is extremely limited.
- Overall gender split is about **71% male / 29% female**.
- Attrition cannot be measured reliably because termination and exit data are missing.

## Recommendations

- Rebalance headcount to reduce overdependence on Production.
- Use forecast-led hiring instead of reactive hiring cycles.
- Develop cross-functional mobility paths between Production, QA, and Engineering.
- Set measurable diversity goals across leadership, technical roles, and production/control functions.
- Improve HR data by adding termination date, exit reason, transfer reason, and standardized date formats.
